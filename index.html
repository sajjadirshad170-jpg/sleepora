Privacy Policy

Sleepora

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

Database Programs ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Sleepora mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how Sleepora handles information when you use the application.

In short: Sleepora is designed to work completely offline. The app does not require an account, does not use a backend, and does not intentionally transmit your sleep schedule information to a server.

1. Information We Collect

Sleepora does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information.

The app allows you to select a bedtime and wake-up time so that it can calculate the duration between those times.

2. Sleep Schedule Information

Bedtime and wake-up times selected within Sleepora are processed locally on your device.

Sleepora does not intentionally transmit your selected sleep schedule information to a remote server.

Sleep duration calculations are performed locally within the app.

3. Account and Backend

Sleepora does not require an account or user registration.

Sleepora does not use Firebase, Firebase Authentication, Firestore, or any cloud database.

Sleepora does not use a backend server for its core functionality.

4. Health and Medical Information

Sleepora does not require or collect health data.

The application does not access medical information, medical records, heart rate information, sleep stages, wearable device information, or other health information.

Sleepora is a simple time-duration calculator and does not provide medical advice.

5. Device Permissions

Sleepora is designed to operate without dangerous Android permissions.

The application does not require access to:

  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • SMS
  • Phone information
  • Bluetooth
  • Calendar
  • Photos or external storage
  • Health information

6. Local Storage

Sleepora may store your selected appearance preference locally on your device.

This preference is used only to remember whether you selected Light, Dark, or System Default appearance.

Sleepora does not use cloud synchronization for this information.

7. Data Sharing

Sleepora does not sell, rent, or intentionally share your personal information with third parties.

Because the core functionality operates locally on your device, your bedtime and wake-up selections are not intentionally sent to our servers.

8. Third-Party Services

Sleepora does not use Firebase, analytics services, advertising services, authentication services, cloud databases, or backend services for its core functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Sleepora does not knowingly collect personal information from children or require users to provide personal information.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us unintentionally, please contact us using the email address below.

10. Data Security

Since Sleepora does not require an account or transmit sleep schedule information to a server for its core functionality, there is no server-side account database containing your sleep schedule data.

Users should keep their Android device secured with appropriate device-level security measures.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
