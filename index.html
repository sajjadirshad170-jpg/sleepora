Privacy Policy

Sleepora

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Database Programs ("we", "us", or "our") developed and operates Sleepora ("the App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Sleepora.

Privacy at a glance: Sleepora is designed as a simple offline sleep-duration calculation application. The App does not require an account, does not use a backend, and does not require users to provide personal information to use its core functionality.

1. Information We Collect

Sleepora does not require users to create an account, register, or provide personal information to use the App's core features.

Sleepora allows users to select a bedtime and wake-up time to calculate the duration between those times.

Information used by the App may include:

  • Selected bedtime
  • Selected wake-up time
  • App appearance preference such as Light, Dark, or System Default

Sleepora does not intentionally collect or transmit this information to Database Programs.

2. How Information Is Used

Information entered into Sleepora is used only to provide the requested sleep-duration calculation.

For example, the selected bedtime and wake-up time are used to calculate the duration between the two selected times.

The calculation is performed locally on your device.

3. Local Processing

Sleepora is designed to perform its core calculations locally on your device.

Your bedtime and wake-up time information are not intentionally uploaded to a Database Programs server.

Database Programs does not maintain a remote database containing your Sleepora sleep schedule information.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally share information entered into Sleepora with third parties.

Sleepora does not require users to submit personal information to use its main functionality.

5. Internet Access

The core sleep-duration calculation functionality of Sleepora is designed to work without an internet connection.

Sleepora does not require internet access for selecting times or calculating sleep duration.

If the App opens an external webpage, such as this Privacy Policy, the Android system or browser may use an internet connection to access that webpage.

6. Permissions

Sleepora does not require dangerous device permissions for its core functionality.

Sleepora does not require access to:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • SMS
  • Phone
  • Bluetooth
  • Calendar
  • Photos or media
  • Health information

The App uses the standard Android time picker for selecting bedtime and wake-up time.

7. Health and Medical Information

Sleepora does not require or collect health data.

The App does not access medical information, medical records, heart rate information, sleep stages, wearable device data, or other health information.

Sleepora is a simple sleep-duration calculator and does not provide medical advice or medical recommendations.

8. Data Storage

Sleepora does not require a cloud database or remote account for its core functionality.

The App may store the user's selected appearance preference locally on the device.

This preference is used only to remember the selected theme setting.

Sleepora does not use cloud synchronization for this information.

9. Data Deletion

Because Sleepora does not require a user account or remote database for its core functionality, there is no online account that needs to be deleted.

Any locally stored application preference can be removed by clearing the App's data through Android device settings or by uninstalling Sleepora, subject to Android device and backup behavior.

10. Data Retention

Database Programs does not maintain a remote copy of bedtime, wake-up time, or sleep-duration calculation information entered into Sleepora.

Any locally stored application preferences remain on your device until they are removed through the applicable device or application controls.

11. Security

We design Sleepora to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of user information.

Since the core functionality is performed locally, sleep schedule calculations do not need to be transmitted to a Database Programs server.

However, no electronic storage or processing system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should use appropriate security features available on their devices.

12. Children's Privacy

Sleepora does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its core functionality.

13. Third-Party Services

The core version of Sleepora does not use Firebase, Firebase Authentication, Firestore, analytics services, a remote backend, or a cloud database for its sleep-duration calculation functionality.

The App is designed to perform its core functionality locally and offline.

If third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be reviewed and updated as necessary to accurately describe the relevant data practices.

14. External Applications

Certain optional actions may open applications provided by other companies, such as a web browser or email application.

Those external applications operate according to their own privacy policies and terms.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Sleepora, applicable requirements, or our practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

16. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, suggestions, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us.

Database Programs

Email: databaseprogramsllc@gmail.com

Application: Sleepora

17. Consent

By using Sleepora, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and the way information is handled as described above.
